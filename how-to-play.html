How to Play

Get started with Qyro Games in seconds — no downloads, no sign-ups!

1

Browse the Game Library

Open Qyro Games on your phone, tablet, or computer. You will see a beautiful grid of all available games. Use the category filters at the top to browse by genre — Action, Puzzle, Casual, and more!

2

Tap a Game to Start

Simply tap or click on any game card that catches your eye. The game will load instantly right in your browser — no app store, no download, no waiting. It is that simple!

3

Play with Touch or Keyboard

All games on Qyro Games are fully optimized for both touch screens and desktop keyboards. On mobile, use tap and swipe gestures. On desktop, use arrow keys, WASD, mouse clicks, or whatever the game requires.

4

Your Progress is Saved

Most games automatically save your high scores and progress using your browser's local storage. The next time you come back, your scores will still be there — no account needed!

5

Explore & Have Fun!

There are no limits! Every game is completely free to play, forever. Explore different genres, challenge your high scores, and enjoy hundreds of hours of entertainment on any device!

Quick Tips

  • Add Qyro Games to your Home Screen for an app-like experience on mobile.
  • Play in landscape mode on your phone for a wider view in action games.
  • Avoid private/incognito mode if you want your game scores saved between sessions.
